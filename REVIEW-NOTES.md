# BuildWorks – Team Review (Oct 25, 2026)

## Must-fix
1. **The game stops after one job.** After payday there's no next contract, so any machine you buy never gets used. An owned machine should also save you the rental cost.
2. **Hiring crew skips the game.** Paying a crew finishes the whole stage instantly, so the cheapest way to win is to never drive. Crew should speed the work up or only finish what's left.
3. **Replaying a task is a money trick.** Anything you spend while replaying gets refunded when it finishes, and replaying too much can leave you too broke to continue. Fix that, and add a backup like a small loan.
4. **Saves can break the game.** A bad or old save isn't checked when it loads, and nothing warns you when saving fails.

## Improvements
- The middle of the job is mostly driving the lowboy back and forth. Let it carry two machines at once, or pay for delivery.
- Give each crew member a real trade-off, such as faster but pricier.
- Add a concrete truck you back up to the forms for the pour.
- Make it run lighter on phones: cheaper shadows, lower screen sharpness on small phones, and pausing when the app is in the background.

## Best next features
- A board with two or three contracts (a driveway, a garage, a bigger house) that use the dozer and roller.
- Deadlines or weather, with a bonus for finishing early.

## Next job idea: Paved (blacktop) driveway
- Today's driveway job is a gravel driveway: the loader spreads crushed stone. That's realistic without a paver.
- Add a later "Paved Driveway" job, or a "Pave the driveway" step after the gravel.
- The player rents or buys a small asphalt paver and drives it down the driveway to lay the hot blacktop.
- Then they use the roller to press it flat. That finally gives the roller a reason to buy.
- A paved driveway should pay more than a gravel one.
- Until a job uses them, hide the dozer and roller from the shop.
- Small detail to decide: replays are free, but re-renting machines or reordering concrete during a replay still costs money (about $4,200 if you replay everything).
